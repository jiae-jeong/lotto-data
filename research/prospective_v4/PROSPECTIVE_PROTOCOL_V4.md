Numbers are six distinct integers 1..45, sorted. Node-score models choose top six, descending scores then lower-number ties. Candidate pool: top six and all 36 one-number substitutions from ranked positions 7..12, deduplicated, sorted by sum of that model's node scores, then lexicographic tuple, limit 37. S chooses top 37 from 10,000 unique uniform bank tuples, seed 2026100901, Python 3.12 random.Random.sample, bank sorted lexicographically; eight feature weights all 1 and support sizes fixed in RULES. Each mode forms one research portfolio: one line from G, then C, then S. In fixed order choose the highest-ranked distinct ticket with pair intersection <=2 to earlier lines.
